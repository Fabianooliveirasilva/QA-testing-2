# AI Output Review Checklist

Complete before any AI-generated content enters `docs/` or the test suite. Human QA approval is mandatory.

- [ ] Every expected result was checked against the real application or is marked **Judgement**.
- [ ] No invented execution results, versions, logs or screenshots.
- [ ] Evidence and hypotheses are clearly separated.
- [ ] No duplicated scenarios; IDs are unique and follow the project convention.
- [ ] Priority and severity are both present and reasoned independently.
- [ ] No credentials or personal data.
- [ ] Wording is clear, English, and reproducible by another tester.
